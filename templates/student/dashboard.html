POLICY NOTICE

All lost and found items, received by Faculty and Staff shall be properly documented, recorded, and securely stored upon receipt.

Items will be retained for one (1) week from the date of receipt to allow the rightful owner sufficient time to claim them. Unclaimed items may be handled in accordance with the institution's Lost and Found policies and procedures.

Students, Faculty, Staff, and other members of the school community are encouraged to promptly report and claim lost items through the Lost and Found System.
